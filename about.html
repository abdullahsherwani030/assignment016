About Zaiqa Mahal

Our Story

Zaiqa Mahal started in 2015 with a simple idea: bring authentic, flavorful food to Gulshan-e-Iqbal. What began as a small takeaway has now become one of Karachi's favorite family restaurants. Every recipe is crafted with love and inspired by traditional Pakistani flavors.

Mission

To serve delicious, hygienic, and affordable food that brings families and friends together. We believe good food creates good memories.

Vision

To become Karachi's most loved neighborhood restaurant, known for consistency, quality, and heartfelt hospitality.

Why Choose Us

  • Fresh Ingredients: We source vegetables and meat daily from trusted local vendors.
  • Best Quality Food: No compromise on taste or hygiene. Our kitchen is ISO certified.
  • Fast Delivery: Hot food delivered to your doorstep in under 45 minutes.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Your feedback drives us. 4.8/5 rating on FoodPanda.
